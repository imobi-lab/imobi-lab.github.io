iMOBI Lab

Intelligent MOBIle Systems Laboratory

Intelligent systems that sense, connect, compute, and act on the move.

We design intelligent mobile systems that bring together wireless networks, edge computing, AI, and cyber-physical systems on UAVs, vehicles, robots, and other connected platforms. We focus on end-to-end system performance, including how hardware and software work together.
