## Bio

Dr. Zhang builds intelligent mobile systems where wireless networking, edge computing, and AI meet, with an emphasis on system-level performance and hardware/software integration. Dr. Zhang received a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of North Texas in 2024 and was named UNT's Outstanding Graduate Student. The dissertation work in UNT's NSF-funded Communication and Signal Processing Laboratory produced an open, networked airborne computing platform: an SDR-based LTE base station carried by a UAV. As a research assistant in the Dynamical Networks and Control lab at UT Arlington, Dr. Zhang also designed a sub-6 GHz-assisted mmWave scheduler for vehicular networks in a Toyota-sponsored project.

Before doctoral study, Dr. Zhang spent 14 years as a software engineer in the telecommunications industry. In that role, Dr. Zhang designed network-side intelligent services that detected and blocked fraudulent calls for a user base of 300 million, earned four patents, and received two Innovation Awards. Dr. Zhang taught at Taylor University before joining UT Tyler in 2025.
