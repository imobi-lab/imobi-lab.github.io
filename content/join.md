## Intro

Students at every level are welcome. Most students come in knowing only part of the stack. What matters most is curiosity and a willingness to get a real system working end to end.

## What you'll work on

Projects pair a research question with a working prototype: a drone, an edge computer, a radio, or an AI-driven tool. Students are encouraged to co-author papers and present at conferences.

## Helpful background

- Python, or C/C++ for embedded work
- Networking, signals, or computer architecture courses
- Machine learning or LLM experience
- Linux, ROS, or hardware tinkering

## UT Tyler undergraduates

You can join through independent study, research credit, or a senior design project. Freshmen and sophomores are welcome. Starting early gives you time to lead a project.

## How to reach out

Email Dr. Zhang with the subject `[iMOBI] Prospective student`. Attach your CV and unofficial transcript, and in a short paragraph name the research theme that interests you most and why.
